## 1. Minimum decision profile

Ask only unanswered questions that can change the result, preferably in one compact batch:

1. Where is the user resident/taxable, and what currency funds the goal?
2. What exact goal and amount are being protected or pursued?
3. When might the money be needed? Is the date flexible?
4. Is there a separate emergency reserve, and how many months of essential spending does it cover?
5. Are income, debt payments, and major near-term expenses stable?
6. What temporary decline could the user watch without selling, and what permanent loss would damage the goal?
7. What products and concentrated exposures are already held?
8. What restrictions matter: capital protection, daily liquidity, ESG/religious constraints, cross-border access, tax, or complexity?

Do not let a user select “high risk tolerance” without testing capacity. Use a concrete loss illustration, such as how a 30% decline on the proposed amount affects the goal and whether the user could wait through a multi-year recovery. Do not assume past calm-market behavior predicts crisis behavior.

## 2. Fit logic

Evaluate in this order:

1. **Goal match:** Does the product's economic function serve the goal—capital stability, liquidity, income, inflation protection, or long-term growth?
2. **Horizon match:** Could a normal adverse cycle still be unresolved when funds are needed?
3. **Loss-capacity match:** Would drawdown, default, surrender loss, or currency loss impair essential plans?
4. **Liquidity match:** Can the user exit at a fair value when needed?
5. **Currency and inflation match:** Is the product aligned with future spending?
6. **Complexity and behavior match:** Can the user understand, monitor, and hold it through expected stress?
7. **Portfolio match:** Does it diversify or duplicate existing factors?
8. **Implementation quality:** Are fees, tracking, tax, spread, issuer, and product structure acceptable relative to alternatives?

Return one of four labels:

- `适合`: material requirements are met and no major conflict is known.
- `有条件适合`: useful only if named conditions hold, such as a long horizon or a strict allocation cap.
- `暂不适合`: a known conflict exists, such as near-term cash need versus deep drawdown risk.
- `信息不足`: missing facts can reverse the conclusion.

The label is a decision aid, not a regulatory suitability determination.

## 3. Default full-report structure

Adapt the depth to the request. A comprehensive answer should follow this shape:

### 先说结论

In two to four sentences, explain what the product is good for, the dominant return engine, the dominant failure mode, and the provisional fit.

### 这项产品实际上持有什么

Identify the exact instrument, underlying exposure, wrapper, currency, leverage/hedging, maturity/liquidity, and who owes or generates the cash flow.

### 收益从哪里来

Give a plain-language money path, then a small return bridge such as:

`underlying total return + FX effect − fees − tracking/friction = investor return before personal tax`

Explain which variables matter and why.

### 风险如何变成亏损

Use a compact table:

| Trigger | What changes | How it reaches the product | Investor consequence | Mitigant and limit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

Rank risks by relevance instead of alphabetically. Include a realistic bad case; distinguish temporary drawdown, permanent loss, and illiquidity.

### 历史上发生过什么

Use a table with consistent data:

| Period / metric | Product | Comparator | Interpretation |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| Common-period annualized return |  |  |  |
| Maximum drawdown (peak → trough) |  |  |  |
| Recovery date/time |  |  |  |
| Annualized volatility |  |  |  |
| Worst calendar year |  |  |  |

Immediately below it, state exact dates, currency, return convention, distribution treatment, frequency, fees/taxes, data sources, and proxy limitations. Describe one or two major historical episodes, but do not imply that they bound future losses.

### 高风险收益溢价与低风险保护来自哪里

Compare a genuinely relevant lower-risk product and, when useful, a higher-risk product. Explain the extra risk being borne, why compensation may exist, when it can disappear, and what the safer product sacrifices in return, liquidity, inflation protection, or upside.

### 与用户的匹配

State the fit label, reasons, minimum conditions, disqualifying conditions, and missing facts. If giving a shortlist, include no more candidates than can be meaningfully distinguished.

### 行动前核对

List only concrete checks: latest prospectus/factsheet, exact share class, fees and spread, redemption/lock-up, guarantee wording, tax and access, and whether the expected holding period survives the adverse scenario.

Close with: `以上用于教育与决策辅助，不构成收益承诺；历史表现不代表未来。采取行动前请核对最新产品文件及适用的当地规则。`

## 4. Short answer mode

When the user asks a narrow question, do not force the entire template. Still preserve the causal chain, exact product identity, data date/source for numbers, and the most important fit caveat.

## 5. Recommendation guardrails

- Do not optimize a shortlist around past return alone.
- Do not use maximum drawdown as the only loss estimate; add a mechanism-based stress scenario.
- Do not call an ETF diversified merely because it holds many securities; inspect sector, country, factor, and top-holding concentration.
- Do not equate a regulator risk label or volatility bucket with the user's capacity.
- Do not treat deposit insurance, a guarantee, or a target payout as universal; verify jurisdiction, cap, eligible holder, maturity, currency, and issuer.
- Do not recommend borrowing, leverage, or complex derivatives to recover losses.
- If the user shows signs of fraud exposure, promised returns, urgency, unlicensed solicitation, or unclear custody, prioritize verification and capital protection over product ranking.
